# Style DNA

## In one line

Pure white, minimalist, hand-drawn, lots of white space, restrained, bizarre, product-sketch feel, handwritten-Chinese feel, clear structure but not an instruction manual.

Like an explanatory sketch casually drawn on blank paper by someone who has long worked on AI, products, design, and dev tools.

## Must

- 16:9 landscape body illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradient, shadow, noise, or vintage-paper feel.
- Mostly black hand-drawn line art: thin lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector, not heavy outlines.
- Lots of white space: the subject takes about 40%-60% of the canvas, at least 35% blank, ideally one whole quiet area.
- Sparse handwritten Chinese annotations: at most 5-8 spots, each ideally 2-8 characters.
- One image conveys only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Express structure naturally; do not write the structure-type name on the image.

## Color

- Black: subject line art, character, frame lines, structure, primary text, main objects.
- Red: key annotations, problems, emotional points, critical reminders, results.
- Orange: main flow, paths, arrows, automation flow direction, A-to-B movement relationships.
- Blue: supplementary notes, mental state, system state, second-layer explanation, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required in every image. Keep color restrained — better too little than too much.

## Never

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographics.
- No formal flowcharts.
- No course slides.
- No cute cartoon posters.
- No children's illustration.
- No complex architecture diagrams.
- No polished flat illustration.
- No techy UI.
- No realistic app screenshots.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, textures.
- Don't explain every node fully.
- No top-left type title such as "Workflow Flowchart / System Architecture / Common Pitfalls / Roadmap".

## Aesthetic direction

Be bizarre, creative, interesting, clean, and wildly imaginative.

Not cute, not childish, not over-complex, not stiff.
