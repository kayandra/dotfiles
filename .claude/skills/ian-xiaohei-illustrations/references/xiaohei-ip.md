# The Xiaohei IP

## Character definition

Xiaohei (小黑) is the fixed visual IP of Ian's Chinese body illustrations.

By default Xiaohei appears in every image. Xiaohei is not a mascot, not a sticker, not cute decoration — it is an absurd worker earnestly taking part in the running of the system.

## Look

- Small solid-black creature.
- White round-dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- The body can be a cylinder, black bean, black box, funnel, shadow, hole, or a black block inside a machine.
- Slightly irregular outline, with a hand-drawn feel.
- Expression blank, dazed, calm, earnest.

## Personality

- Very earnest, but doing something a bit absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Deadpan humor, no acting cute.
- A bit clumsy, but not dumb.
- Like it's genuinely in charge of some job inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Common duties

Let Xiaohei carry the core action:

- Hauling material.
- Pulling lines to converge information sources.
- Stuck inside a breakpoint.
- Operating the "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a filtering funnel.
- Cutting open the "material fish".
- Stamping handoff copy.
- Leading along a handoff path.
- Holding a warning sign while watching a pit.
- Reaching out of a hole but failing to catch the content.
- Off to the side hauling bricks, building bridges, opening doors, sorting, recording.

## Forbidden

- Don't draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Don't make it a children's cartoon character.
- Don't give Xiaohei complex clothing, emoji faces, or shiny eyes.
- Don't let Xiaohei just stand in a corner watching.
- Don't let Xiaohei steal the structural expression.
- Don't draw Xiaohei too commercial, too rounded, too polished.

## Test

If you remove Xiaohei and the image's core metaphor still fully holds, then Xiaohei is too decorative; rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the subject of the action.
